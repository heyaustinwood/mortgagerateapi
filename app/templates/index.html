Where the numbers come from. Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS), published every Thursday. We pull it from FRED and keep the full history, so every answer is the same number the news reports.

Quickstart

Subscribe to get a key, then export it:

export MORTGAGE_RATE_KEY="mra_..."

Rates over a time period

curl -s "https://mortgagerateapi.com/v1/rates?term=30&start=2024-01-01&end=2024-03-31" \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $MORTGAGE_RATE_KEY" {"term": 30, "start": "2024-01-01", "end": "2024-03-31", "rates": [{"date": "2024-01-04", "rate": 6.62}, {"date": "2024-01-11", "rate": 6.66}, ...], "source": "Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS), via FRED"}

One entry per week, oldest first. Leave out end for today, and start for one year before end.

The latest rates

curl -s https://mortgagerateapi.com/v1/rates/latest \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $MORTGAGE_RATE_KEY" {"rates": [{"term": 30, "date": "...", "rate": ...}, {"term": 15, "date": "...", "rate": ...}], "source": "..."}

Conventions

  • Base URL is https://mortgagerateapi.com/v1. HTTPS only.
  • Authentication is Authorization: Bearer <key>. Where you can't set headers (a spreadsheet, say), ?api_key=<key> works too.
  • Rates are annual percentages: 6.62 means 6.62%.
  • Errors share one shape, {"error": {"code": "...", "message": "..."}}, with status 400 for bad input, 401 for a missing or wrong key, and 402 when the subscription has lapsed.
